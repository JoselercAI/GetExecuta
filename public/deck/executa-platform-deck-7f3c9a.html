01 — The Problem
The Problem

Generic AI doesn't work for finance.
And the alternatives don't scale.

Every investment firm faces the same constraint: produce institutional-quality deal output, fast, without compromising on accuracy, methodology, or regulatory standing. The available tools each fail on a different axis.

01 — Regulatory Exposure
ChatGPT & Copilot create risk
Documents sent to generic AI may constitute unlawful disclosure under MAR, breach GDPR, and violate client NDAs. Compliance teams can't approve them on live deal data.
02 — Data Stuck in Silos
Your firm's data isn't reachable
CRM, deal files, past memos, data rooms — every system holds part of the picture. No tool reads across all of them at once, so analysts rebuild context every time.
03 — No Firm-Private Workspace
Public tools aren't approval-grade
Without an isolated, firm-private workspace, no AI tool can be deployed on live deal data. Building one in-house takes 18+ months of engineering, security, and ongoing maintenance.
04 — Analyst Cost
Linear cost, non-linear constraints
Adding analysts at €80–120K each scales linearly, but the bottleneck is senior review capacity. More analysts means more drafts to check — not more deals signed.
05 — Brand & Firm Voice
Generic AI dilutes the partner
Off-the-shelf AI produces off-the-shelf prose. In a business where the partner is the product, every CIM, memo, and pitch must read in the firm's voice — not a model's default.
06 — Productivity Gap
Falling behind firms that have AI
Firms that have institutional AI run mandates in days that used to take weeks. Without daily AI woven into the workflow, the productivity gap compounds with every deal cycle.
THE WEDGE
You need institutional-grade AI that's safe to use on live deal data, that produces the deliverable, and that compounds in value over time.
